CIBIL score

Understand your credit score. Strengthen your financial future.

A CIBIL score is a three-digit number from 300 to 900 that summarises your credit history. Lenders look at it when deciding whether, and on what terms, to offer you credit.

Talk to us about my score

We do not issue official credit scores. You can get yours directly from an authorised credit bureau.

  1. Payment historyPaying EMIs and card bills on time matters most.
  2. Credit utilisationKeeping card usage well below your limit helps.
  3. Length of credit historyOlder, well-managed accounts build trust.
  4. Credit mixA healthy blend of secured and unsecured credit.
  5. New applicationsMany applications in a short time can lower your score.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

What types of loans does Aura Finkart offer?

We help with personal, home, business, loan against property, auto, machinery, education and gold loans, along with insurance and investment guidance.

How can I apply for a personal loan?

Send an enquiry through the form, call us or message us on WhatsApp. Our team will review your requirement and guide you through lender options.

What documents are required for a loan?

Requirements differ by loan type and lender. Typically this includes identity proof, address proof, income documents and bank statements. We will share the exact list for your case.

How is loan eligibility determined?

Lenders consider factors such as income, credit history, existing obligations, age and the type of loan. The final decision is made by the lender.

What is a CIBIL score?

It is a three-digit number between 300 and 900 that summarises your credit history. A higher score generally improves your chances with lenders.

How does an EMI calculator work?

It applies the reducing-balance formula EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), where P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly instalments.

Can I compare different loan options?

Yes. We help you understand suitable lender options so you can compare rates, tenure and terms before deciding.

How long does loan processing take?

It depends on the loan type, documents and lender. We will give you a realistic estimate once your requirement is reviewed.

Are loan approvals guaranteed?

No. Approval and disbursement are decided solely by the bank or NBFC after assessing your application.

How can I contact a financial advisor?

Call us, message us on WhatsApp or fill in the enquiry form below. An advisor will get back to you.
